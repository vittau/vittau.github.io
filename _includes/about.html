Ten years on the web, a doctorate in logic, and a camera never far away.

Most of my career has been on the web. Before Ferreira Costa — Brazil’s second-largest home improvement retailer, where I also help shape the upstream and downstream frameworks other teams build on — I spent nearly four years as tech lead and engineering manager at Passei Direto, a learning platform with more than 20 million study materials, and led a team of fourteen at a health tech startup through the pandemic.

My doctorate is in Artificial Intelligence at PESC/COPPE (UFRJ), on temporal logics for social networks, published in the Journal of Logic and Computation. Brazilian Portuguese is my native language; my English is advanced in writing and speaking (TOEFL ITP 667/677). Away from the keyboard I shoot RAW and draw vector art.
